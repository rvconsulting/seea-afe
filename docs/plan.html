Mauritius Water Accounts — Source Verification (May 2026)

The two accounts recorded for Mauritius in the AFE 2025 assessment (“Physical supply and use tables for water” and “Physical asset accounts for water”) are confirmed to exist and are publicly accessible. They are both contained in the same report:

Primary publication

Statistics Mauritius — Water Account, Mauritius 2019–2020 (4th edition, published November 2022)

  • SEEA.un.org entry: https://seea.un.org/content/water-account-mauritius-2019-2020
  • Direct PDF (via SEEA.un.org): water_yr20_301122.pdf (1.86 MB)
    • Likely stable URL: https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/By_Subject/Energy_Water/water_yr20_301122.pdf
  • Statistics Mauritius homepage: https://statsmauritius.govmu.org → By Subject → Energy & Water
  • Author: Statistics Mauritius, Ministry of Finance, Economic Planning and Development
  • Content: Physical supply and use tables (2019–2020) + Water Asset Account (Table 6) + trend data 2011–2020
  • This is the 4th consecutive edition — confirming the sustained programme that justifies Mauritius’s Stage III classification; the accounts are real and institutionalized but were not shared with the review team.

Why they were missing from our repository

The documents exist on the UN SEEA platform and the Statistics Mauritius website, but they were apparently not included in the OneDrive project folder shared with this team. They should be downloaded and added to data/assessment/mus/ for completeness.

Supplementary resource — NAO 2026 water audit

A February 2026 National Audit Office (Mauritius) report directly validates the UFW finding discussed in Section 4 (Water Accounts — Mauritius):

  • URL: https://nao.govmu.org/nao/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/water_report.pdf
  • Confirms: NRW averaged 60% island-wide in 2024, consistent with the 64.6% figure from the 2019–2020 accounts
  • Quantifies: ~195 million m³ of treated water lost or unaccounted annually; cumulative Central Water Authority (CWA) deficit of Rs 3.9 billion (2015–2025)
  • This report can be cited alongside [@statsmauritius_water_2024] for the MUS.water.3 inventory item.

Potential extension to Figure 1 (Water Productivity)

An earlier Mauritius water account presentation (SADC Economic Accounting of Water, available via SEEA.un.org presentations) reports sector-level water productivity for Mauritius: - Agriculture: USD 0.38/m³ - Industry: USD 236.74/m³
- Economy average: USD 27.41/m³ - Agriculture uses 67.7% of water but contributes only 6% of GDP

This would add Mauritius as a third country in Figure 1 (currently only Rwanda and Uganda), and would strengthen the cross-country water productivity comparison. The data would need to be sourced from the published accounts or the SADC presentation; ask the team if this dataset is available in the OneDrive.
